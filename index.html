Find your way.
Plan your next fishing trip.

World GPS combines live street maps, optional offline country downloads and a fishing dashboard. The upcoming public release is free and ad-free.

Android testing

The app is being prepared for Google Play. It is not yet published there. Android test builds and country road packages are available on GitHub. Use only the installation instructions for the build you download.

Maps when you need them

A fresh installation contains no country maps. Use the live map with an internet connection, or choose an available country in Offline Maps. Wait for maps, search and driving data to finish preparing before travelling offline.

Fishing information

Choose a fishing location to explore hourly weather, wind, coastal waves, sea temperature, daylight and model tide estimates. Coverage varies by location. Fishing windows are suggestions, not catch predictions. Tide estimates do not replace official local tide tables or navigation charts.

Your journal stays with you

Keep private spots, catch notes and selected photos on your phone. Export important content before uninstalling. Read the privacy policy.
